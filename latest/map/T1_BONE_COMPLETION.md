Once the concentration is understood his selection is relatively predictable. No target or attack after his approved +56 strike is selected here.

**BONE = ATTENTION.** He is extremely fast, intelligent, precise, maliciously playful and readily redirected by a meaningful stimulus. Movement, hiding, sound, a shutting door, a watcher, a protective gesture, an interesting position, familiarity or loss of interest can change his focus. He may stalk, observe, pursue, allow flight/hiding, change angle, deliberately threaten without hitting a person, or abandon the encounter. He does not systematically clear households, follow the largest crowd or maximize kills. **When Bone intends to hit something, he does not miss. Apparent near-misses are deliberate.**

**BARRIER = BOUNDARY / REACTIVE WEAPON.** Formation remains +0–60; physical seal at +60 is still translucent; darkening continues to effective opacity at +90.

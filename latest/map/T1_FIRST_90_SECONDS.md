## 2. Bone's first victims

**M01 is the existing two-person handcart group within P05**, already returning home on the farm approach. Its original anchor is unchanged at map (−1.1, −14.9) m. The cellar landing is about 13 m away in a straight line; the short usable approach is about 15 m as the cart advances. Bone can cover it with a brief fast burst, reaching the first worker at +4 and the shocked helper at +6.

This is earlier than the example +15–30 window because waiting would be artificial at this distance and could let the visibly emerging Blood warn them first. Neither worker is assigned flight before the attack. The other four P05 people are not conveniently hidden: they recoil about eight metres and shout. This starts a **small lower-village warning**, not village-wide knowledge.

The first two stars mark attack events, not final body positions. Bone is last confirmed at map (−4.6, −5.5) m at +12. A footprint-clipped local cellar/gate-side pocket is shown for the remainder of this opening. His precise movements inside cover after +12 are **not resolved**. This avoids inventing an onward hunt, but it also means there is no exact Bone pose at +90 yet. It requires a decision before Step 3; it is not a claim that he stands still for 78 seconds.

## 3. Blood's first target

The natural target is **P08's outdoor well and market activity**, at the civic road junction. It is the largest nearby public outdoor concentration on the existing route. Blood takes the cellar access → Witch Lane → main street, without diverting toward the tavern.

The route measures **88.50 m in the saved road's height profile** (87.98 m in plan). Departure at +2 and arrival at +54 require **1.70 m/s**. This is a purposeful long, heavy stride, followed by a two-second strike preparation. If Blood must instead shamble substantially slower, this first major event will have to occur later; the geography should not be compressed to compensate.

Five of P08's 26 people depart normally before the strike: M04's two water carriers start at +22 at 0.75 m/s, and three ordinary passers walk to the older homes from +10–39. **Twenty-one remain in the centre core.** The first strike intersects the other two well users and six people using the stall positions. Working accounting is four fatalities (two well users, two stall people) and four nonfatal injuries (remaining stall people). The other 13 core people react locally. The five departed people remain part of the P08 origin ledger and survive this window.

The impact is a small editable event footprint across existing well/stall positions. One light frame or fixture failing establishes visible disruption and a heavy crash. No house collapse, final broken asset, corpse placement, second attack or later injury outcome is authored. The 16 × 6 m zone is a planning envelope, not a final attack ability specification.

**Nearby reception:** porch patrons have a short open view; the tavern roof masks the service-side group and interior. Open plan-view rays toward the family yard and church threshold make direct recognition possible there. Smith and upper-house rays meet building footprints, so those groups rely more on noise, calls and changing light. These are 2D visibility checks, not UE raycasts. Sound reaches nearby areas quickly; the delay is in recognition and relay, not an unrealistically slow sound wave.

## 5. Barrier formation

The proposed spell rim encloses the existing fields, roads, buildings, cemetery reserve and estate, with a margin around the inhabited clearing. It also includes the recorded forest working edge and the approach corridor down toward the woodland entrance. It does not replace the forest edge or create a new fence/terrain boundary.

The rim is about **903.7 m** long. Its seed, on the western outer edge nearest the Witch's property, is approximately (−49.35, −14.47) m. Two fronts travel in opposite directions at about **7.53 m/s**, a magical propagation rate. Half the perimeter is formed at +30. The final rim seam is on the eastern residential edge, approximately (+68.73, +36.92) m, at +60.

While the fronts travel, translucent walls rise and canopy strands converge above the village. Unformed perimeter arcs and the overhead opening remain physically incomplete. At +60 the last seam and crown connect. The exact three-dimensional crown height is schematic and must later clear the hill, buildings and trees; it is not being implemented now.

## 8. Spatial/narrative findings and limits

- **Bone timing:** the actual cellar-to-handcart distance supports very early violence. The sparse location is not empty; surviving P05 witnesses make total silence impossible. Warnings spread locally without making the whole settlement aware.
- **Blood timing:** the centre is reachable within the window at a purposeful 1.70 m/s. A substantially slower movement style requires a later strike. The tavern's adjacency limits how long its outdoor and indoor populations can remain unaware.
- **Uneven knowledge:** plan-view sight, roof obstructions, ordinary interior noise, physical relays and early western-rim formation explain differences. A definitive 3D sound/visibility simulation has not been performed.
- **Bone after +12 remains bounded but unresolved.** His local pocket is preserved for review, rather than pretending to know his exact hidden actions for the remaining 78 seconds. No further kills are silently assigned. Step 3 needs an agreed pose/action within that pocket.
- **Within-cluster detail:** T0 did not give every unlabelled person an exact point. The orchard worker is placed at the existing produce basket within its original orchard assignment; well/stall membership follows T0 subdivisions. These are explicit refinements, not moved population clusters. All named T0 micro-group anchors remain exact at +0.
- **Motion precision:** actor height samples use the saved road profile. Ordinary civilian trips use plan distances and working finish/departure times, not a crowd solver. No path crosses a building/outbuilding footprint by more than the 0.25 m review tolerance; roof projection and terrain remain navigation caveats.
- **Confinement:** the rim encloses all existing inhabited/agricultural, estate, reserve and road footprints. Its crown and transmission curve are spell-design assumptions, not new approved geography.

The ordinary-life check still holds: workers are finishing, collecting water, returning, delivering and tending households. M04 and three passers leave on normal business before the strike. People were not held or moved into an attack for spectacle. Nothing in this pass assigns eventual fates to the other survivors.
